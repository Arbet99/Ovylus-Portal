## Prompt Utama Google Stitch

```text
Create a high-fidelity, mobile-first, responsive homepage for “Ovylus Portal”, an Indonesian AI media and discovery platform.

CORE CREATIVE DIRECTION
Combine two different design references with clearly separated roles:

1. Use the uploaded “desing 1.jpg” as the structural reference:
- cinematic movie/streaming-platform browsing experience;
- large featured-content hero;
- organized poster-driven content discovery;
- horizontal content rows and structured grids;
- strong visual hierarchy;
- content detail reached by selecting a poster.

2. Use the KINETIC SMM Studio reference as the visual-language reference:
- editorial Pure White and Deep Black composition;
- bold oversized sans-serif typography;
- exact Kinetic Orange accent;
- selective monochrome imagery;
- controlled motion blur, light streaks, speed, and visual energy;
- clean rounded cards;
- confident use of whitespace;
- modern, bold, premium, and dynamic atmosphere.

Do not copy either design literally. Do not create an agency portfolio. Do not create a movie-ticket website. Create a distinctive AI media portal that uses a streaming catalog structure and a KINETIC editorial identity.

PRODUCT
- Brand: Ovylus.
- Product: Ovylus Portal.
- Interface language: Indonesian.
- Purpose: help users discover AI news, AI videos, automation guides, AI education, and AI creators.
- Personality: intelligent, energetic, editorial, futuristic, credible, premium, and easy to explore.

DESIGN SYSTEM
Create two equally polished themes.

OFFICIAL CORE COLOR PALETTE — USE THESE EXACT VALUES
- Pure White: #FBFBFB
  Meaning: clarity, simplicity, minimalism, and breathing space.
- Deep Black: #040404
  Meaning: strength, confidence, contrast, premium character, and visual drama.
- Kinetic Orange: #FF4000
  Meaning: energy, motion, innovation, attention, action, and the dynamic spirit of Ovylus.
- Neutral Grey: #DEDEDE
  Meaning: balance, stability, soft backgrounds, separation, and support for contrast.

Do not replace these colors with approximate warm whites, near-blacks, coral, red-orange, or alternative grays. These four exact colors are the Ovylus Portal core palette. Create depth primarily through opacity, spacing, borders, hierarchy, imagery, and shadows rather than introducing additional brand colors.

COLOR ACCESSIBILITY RULES
- Do not use Kinetic Orange #FF4000 as normal-size body text on Pure White #FBFBFB.
- For orange CTA buttons, prefer Deep Black #040404 text on Kinetic Orange #FF4000.
- Do not use Neutral Grey #DEDEDE as readable text on Pure White #FBFBFB; use it for surfaces, borders, dividers, and decoration.
- Use Deep Black #040404 for primary and secondary readable text in light mode, adjusting hierarchy with weight, size, and opacity only when contrast remains sufficient.
- In dark mode, test Kinetic Orange, Neutral Grey, and Pure White combinations for each text size and interaction state.

LIGHT MODE — DEFAULT
- Main background: Pure White #FBFBFB.
- Primary text and navigation: Deep Black #040404.
- Soft sections, dividers, borders, and secondary surfaces: Neutral Grey #DEDEDE.
- Primary accent, active states, CTA, selected filters, progress, and branding: Kinetic Orange #FF4000.
- Cards may use Pure White with Neutral Grey separation and restrained shadows.

DARK MODE
- Main background: Deep Black #040404.
- Primary text: Pure White #FBFBFB.
- Secondary text, dividers, and borders: Neutral Grey #DEDEDE, using controlled opacity when depth is needed.
- Primary accent remains exactly Kinetic Orange #FF4000.
- Build surface separation with opacity, borders, spacing, imagery, and restrained shadows rather than adding unrelated dark brand colors.

Do not use glassmorphism, translucent glass panels, excessive gradients, purple-blue neon, or generic AI visual clichés.

Use Kinetic Orange #FF4000 selectively for:
- active navigation;
- primary CTA;
- category indicators;
- progress or carousel state;
- small branding marks;
- kinetic streaks and motion elements;
- selected or hover states.

Do not paint every component orange. Preserve strong black, white, and neutral contrast.

TYPOGRAPHY
- Use bold modern sans-serif typography.
- Headlines can be oversized and editorial, inspired by KINETIC.
- Keep card titles compact and easy to scan.
- Use a neutral, readable sans-serif for article summaries and metadata.
- Create clear hierarchy between hero title, section title, card title, category, source, and date.
- Avoid futuristic decorative fonts that reduce readability.

TOP NAVIGATION
Create a clean top navigation bar without glass effects.

Desktop structure:
- Place the Ovylus wordmark/logo on the left.
- Place exactly six primary menu items in the visual center of the header:
  1. Beranda
  2. Berita AI
  3. Video AI
  4. Automation
  5. Belajar AI
  6. Kreator
- Place search and the light/dark theme switcher on the right.
- Show “Beranda” as the active menu using a controlled orange-red accent.
- Keep the six-menu group mathematically and visually centered, independent of the left and right control widths.
- Use generous spacing and precise alignment.

HOMEPAGE

1. FEATURED HERO
- Create one large cinematic featured story.
- Use a strong AI-related image or artwork.
- The image may use selective monochrome treatment with controlled Kinetic Orange motion streaks or blur accents.
- Include content type, category, title, short summary, publication date, source, and reading time.
- Primary CTA: “Baca Selengkapnya”.
- Secondary CTA: “Lihat Sumber” or “Simpan”.
- Add restrained carousel indicators for additional featured stories.
- Keep the hero editorial and cinematic, not like an agency sales banner.

2. DISCOVERY AND FILTERS
- Add clean topic chips or filters near the hero.
- Include: Terbaru, Populer, Model AI, Tools, Bisnis, Riset, and Regulasi.
- Use clean solid or outlined controls; no glass surfaces.
- Make the active filter Kinetic Orange #FF4000.

3. POSTER-DRIVEN CONTENT SECTIONS
Create organized sections using horizontal poster rows and selected grids:
- Berita AI Terbaru
- Video AI Pilihan
- Automation untuk Bisnis
- Belajar AI
- Kreator Pilihan
- Sedang Populer

The page must feel like browsing a premium streaming catalog, but every item must clearly represent AI media content.

4. CONTENT CARDS
- Posters and thumbnails are the main discovery element.
- Use consistent portrait or cinematic image ratios.
- Each card includes a short title, content-type badge, category, and minimal metadata.
- Video cards include a clear play indicator.
- Learning cards may include level or duration.
- Automation cards may include use-case type or required tools.
- Sponsored content must display a visible “Sponsored” or “Promoted” label.
- Use rounded cards, clean borders, subtle shadows, and decisive spacing.
- Use selective Kinetic Orange hover or selected states.
- Do not apply motion blur to every poster. Preserve content-image clarity.

5. CREATOR SECTION
- Display selected AI creators as polished visual profile cards.
- Include avatar, creator name, specialty, platform icon, and featured-content count.
- Include CTA: “Lihat Semua Kreator”.
- Keep the section consistent with the poster-based discovery system.

6. FOOTER
- Use a clean editorial footer.
- Include Ovylus identity, primary links, social links, editorial policy, privacy, and copyright.
- Adapt the footer properly for both light and dark themes.

KINETIC VISUAL ELEMENTS
Use KINETIC-inspired motion language carefully:
- Kinetic Orange #FF4000 directional streaks;
- selective blur on decorative shapes or hero transitions;
- subtle speed trails;
- short energetic transitions;
- monochrome-to-Kinetic-Orange hover accents;
- editorial cropping and strong scale contrast.

Motion must support navigation and brand recognition. It must not make text harder to read, obscure poster images, or reduce mobile performance.

RESPONSIVE BEHAVIOR
Design mobile first, then adapt to tablet and desktop.

Mobile:
- compact header with Ovylus logo, search, theme switcher, and menu trigger;
- do not force all six menu items into one horizontal row;
- expose the six menu items through a clear mobile menu or carefully designed bottom navigation pattern;
- shorter hero with one primary CTA;
- horizontally swipeable poster rows or a two-column poster grid;
- horizontally scrollable category chips;
- comfortable touch targets;
- reduce decorative motion and blur;
- maintain editorial typography without oversized text blocking the content.

Tablet:
- reduce the number of cards per row;
- preserve hierarchy and content labels;
- keep navigation readable without overcrowding.

Desktop:
- centered six-item navigation;
- wide cinematic hero;
- multiple poster cards per row;
- stronger typography scale and more whitespace;
- subtle hover interactions and kinetic transitions.

ACCESSIBILITY AND USABILITY
- Prioritize content discovery and reading comfort.
- Maintain strong text/background contrast in both themes.
- Never rely on orange alone to communicate state.
- Provide visible focus states.
- Support reduced-motion preferences.
- Avoid tiny metadata text.
- Avoid random card placement or uncontrolled masonry layouts.
- Avoid excessive animation, blur, glow, and decorative noise.
- Ensure article-reading pages can use a simpler editorial layout than the homepage.

REALISTIC CONTENT
Use realistic Indonesian AI content instead of movie or agency placeholders. Example themes:
- perkembangan model AI;
- AI agent dan automation;
- tools produktivitas;
- regulasi AI;
- tutorial dan learning paths;
- creator videos about practical AI use.

OUTPUT
Generate:
1. A polished desktop homepage in default light mode.
2. A polished desktop homepage in dark mode.
3. A responsive mobile homepage in default light mode.
4. Reusable components and a coherent design system that can later support article detail, video detail, creator profile, search, and admin dashboard screens.
```

## Prompt Koreksi — Jika Terlihat Seperti Agency

```text
The visual style is too similar to an agency portfolio. Keep the KINETIC color palette, typography, motion language, and editorial energy, but restore a clear media-discovery structure: featured AI story hero, topic filters, organized poster rows, content cards, video cards, learning cards, and creator profiles. The product must feel like an AI media portal with streaming-platform discovery, not a creative agency website.
```

## Prompt Koreksi — Jika Terlihat Terlalu Mirip Movie Website

```text
Keep the cinematic poster-based discovery system, but remove entertainment and cinema semantics. Replace movie titles, genres, ratings, trailers, cast, tickets, and schedules with AI headlines, sources, publication dates, reading time, content type, learning level, tools, creator identity, and article actions. This is an AI media portal, not a movie or ticket-booking service.
```

## Prompt Koreksi — Jika Aksen Oranye Berlebihan

```text
Reduce the amount of Kinetic Orange without changing its exact value #FF4000. Use it only for the active navigation item, primary CTA, selected filters, small badges, carousel progress, and restrained kinetic streaks. Restore Pure White #FBFBFB, Deep Black #040404, and Neutral Grey #DEDEDE as the dominant visual foundation. Do not make entire sections or every card orange.
```

## Prompt Koreksi — Jika Motion Blur Berlebihan

```text
Remove motion blur from content posters, text, and important controls. Keep motion blur only on decorative hero elements, transitional streaks, and subtle brand moments. All content images, article titles, metadata, and navigation must remain sharp and easy to scan.
```
